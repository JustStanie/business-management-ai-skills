# Content Quality for Search and AI-Assisted Discovery

## Purpose

Help people create discoverable, accurate, useful content that answers real audience questions. Evaluate pages using existing SEO fundamentals, clarity, original evidence and user utility. Do **not** claim that ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity or Google AI Overviews have universal citation preferences or guaranteed ranking rules.

## Core Principles

1. **People-first value:** Answer the user's real question with unique and correct information, not filler optimized for bots.
2. **Search foundations:** Ensure pages are crawlable, indexed when applicable, have working links, sensible headings, accurate titles and usable visible text.
3. **Verifiable evidence:** Attribute claims to credible, dated primary sources when possible; never manufacture numerical facts, expertise or quotes.
4. **Appropriate structure:** Use definitions, tables, FAQs, schema, or summaries *only when they genuinely help people* and accurately reflect visible page content.
5. **No guaranteed AI citations:** Different AI systems select and present sources differently; no fixed word count, table placement, fact quota or schema type guarantees inclusion.
6. **No special Google AI markup required:** As of the reviewed official guidance, Google AI Overviews/AI Mode do not require distinct SEO tactics, special JSON-LD or `llms.txt` to appear.

## Assessment Workflow

1. Determine target users, user intent, specific search questions, industry and publication format.
2. Inspect factual accuracy, original contribution, author/source accountability, update needs and any relevant YMYL risks.
3. Audit accessibility and ordinary technical SEO: crawl permissions, canonical/index directives, internal links, text visibility and page experience.
4. Compare competing content to find evidence or perspective gaps, not to copy alleged citation formulas.
5. Improve directness, clarity, useful context, citations, definitions and examples only where needed; keep editorial integrity.
6. Validate structured data against visible content and official Google Search policy; do not insert FAQ markup purely as a ranking shortcut.
7. Establish a measurement plan: Search Console visibility, relevant queries, referral patterns, user engagement and conversion outcomes; if available, compare before and after with seasonality and site changes controlled.
8. Report uncertainty and avoid inferring an AI system's internal source selection rules from anecdotes.

## Practical Output

For an audit, provide:

- The main audience question and the current page's answer quality.
- Three to five prioritized content or technical issues with evidence.
- Proposed improvements and sample text that does not invent facts or endorsements.
- Publisher/documentation requirements that need current verification.
- A measurable experiment or monitoring plan, without promising ranking lift.

For a simple content question, answer directly without a full audit.

## Common Mistakes

- Promising a citation because an answer appears in the first 150 words.
- Treating a specific number of statistics, FAQ items or tables as a citation requirement.
- Fabricating studies or recycling unverified email-marketing ROI figures.
- Inventing author biographies, professional experience or testimonials.
- Assuming FAQ schema yields AI citations or guaranteed rich results.
- Publishing low-value, keyword-stuffed content for generative engines.
- Claiming proprietary knowledge of AI source-selection algorithms.

## Verification Checklist

- Check current Google Search Central guidance before changing SEO or structured data.
- Ensure all external figures have primary sources, dates, units and context.
- Verify technical changes on a staging site and monitor post-deploy effects.
- Distinguish measured performance changes from correlation and seasonality.
- Confirm copyright, privacy and disclosure requirements for published material.

## Technical audit acceptance checks (2026-10-31)

- **Skill-specific objective:** Improve trustworthy, helpful search content without claiming guaranteed generative-AI citations.
- **Domain correctness:** Validate Google Search indexing, page usefulness, original evidence, crawler access and structured-data policy.
- **Failure and assurance:** Do not manufacture benchmarks, endorsements, sources, author qualifications or AI-ranking algorithms.
- **Evidence:** Verify actual performance through measured search/referral data and a controlled assessment of other concurrent changes.
